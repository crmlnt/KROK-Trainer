Introducing Mistake Training 2.0 — Learn From Your Mistakes

A wrong answer can show you exactly where to focus next. Available in stable version 1.9.68, Mistake Training 2.0 turns new Practice mistakes into a personal review library, focused training sessions and a clear record of progress.

From a Practice mistake to your next study session

Sign in to KROK Trainer and study in Practice Mode. When you answer a main-bank question incorrectly, it is registered in your account's Mistake Training archive. You can open Mistake Training directly from the sidebar whenever you want to revisit it; you do not need to finish a Practice session first.

This begins with new mistakes made while signed in. Earlier browser-only mistakes and guest activity are not automatically imported. Past Papers are excluded from Mistake Training.

Review Mistakes and Mistake Training: two different tools

Review Mistakes remains available inside Practice Mode. It lets you revisit saved Practice errors on that browser, without changing your Practice counters. It is useful for a quick review while studying.

Mistake Training is a separate account feature. It keeps your registered questions and mastery progress across visits and devices, and provides structured sessions that you can resume. Reviewing the local archive does not award Mistake Training credits.

Three categories, one clear learning goal

  • Need Review: questions with 0 of 3 mastery credits.
  • In Progress: questions with 1 or 2 credits.
  • Mastered: questions with all 3 credits.

To earn a credit for a question, answer it correctly during a valid training session and finalize that session. A valid session contains 5–50 distinct questions, with every question answered. Each correctly answered question earns one credit when you finalize; the three credits must come from three different completed sessions.

A correct answer is not yet a mastery creditFeedback appears as soon as you answer. Credits are awarded only after you complete and finalize the session. An interrupted, unfinished session does not add credits.

If you answer a question incorrectly again in Practice or Mistake Training, its progress resets to 0/3. Other questions keep their own progress. A Mastered question remains in your library, so you can consult it or include it in a custom session later.

A dashboard that helps you choose what comes next

The dashboard shows Need Review, In Progress, Mastered and Total Questions for the selected KROK. These are your account's distinct registered questions, not a count of errors in the older browser archive. Select a card to open that category, or Total Questions to browse the full library.

Your progress shows the percentage of saved questions that are Mastered. Your next training offers a recommended session of up to ten unmastered questions, prioritizing those that need review. If fewer than five eligible questions are available, the app explains why the recommended session cannot start. Custom Training lets you choose 5–50 questions from your archive.

Browse questions without starting a test

Open Browse Questions to explore the full library or a mastery category. Filter by subject, search question text or an identifier, and choose View Question to read the question and its answers. Each entry shows its subject and current credit count.

Consultation does not submit an answer or change mastery. Questions with similar wording but different identities remain separate entries with their own progress.

Train, pause and pick up where you left off

During training, the app shows your question position, session progress and immediate feedback. Answer choices are shuffled, and their order stays stable when you return to the same session. Move through the questions, then select Finalize session to save the earned credits.

If you refresh, close the page or return later, open Mistake Training while signed in to the same account and choose Resume Training. Accepted answers are saved with the session. If a connection problem occurs, use the displayed retry option to confirm the same request before continuing.

KROK 1 and KROK 2 stay separate

Use the dashboard's Question bank selector to switch between KROK 1 and KROK 2. Each has its own saved questions, progress and active session. Your study results are not combined across the two exams.

Mistake Training complements Statistics / Analytics 2.0: Statistics helps you understand performance in saved Exam Mode sessions, while Mistake Training helps you work through registered Practice mistakes. Together, they support a simple routine: identify a gap, revisit the concept and test your understanding again.
