PRODUCT UPDATE23 August 2026

A smarter KROK Trainer: exam review, Weak Subjects and better statistics

The latest KROK Trainer update turns Exam History into a much more useful study tool and introduces question-level analytics designed to identify where your preparation needs more attention.

Exam History is now more than a score log

New exam sessions now store the individual questions and answers that produced each result. From Exam History, a completed exam can be opened again to review every question, compare the selected answer with the correct answer and filter the session to mistakes only.

A new Retry Wrong Answers action can also create a focused study session using only the mistakes from that exam. Retry sessions are intentionally kept separate from formal exam statistics, so additional practice does not distort performance data.

All Topics exams now contribute to subject statistics

Previously, an exam taken using All Topics was stored as one general session. That meant its questions could not contribute correctly to Performance by Subject. KROK Trainer now analyses the subject attached to each saved question, allowing mixed exams to be distributed across Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry and the other relevant disciplines.

Introducing Weak Subjects

The Statistics dashboard can now use the same question-level data to identify subjects that may need more attention. To avoid drawing conclusions from only one or two questions, a subject needs at least 10 answered questions before receiving a performance classification.

Weak Subjects classificationWeak: below 50% · At risk: 50–63% · On track: 64% or higher. Subjects with fewer than 10 answers remain marked as Not enough data.

The list is collapsible, keeping the dashboard compact even when many subjects are being tracked — particularly useful for KROK 2.

A 64% passing threshold

KROK Trainer's pass/fail logic has also been updated from the previous 60% value to the current 64% minimum threshold used for the KROK exams. Exam results and Exam History now follow the same threshold.

Built on persistent study data

These features share the same underlying improvement: registered users can build a persistent history of individual exam answers. This creates a stronger foundation for future study analytics while keeping KROK Trainer focused on a simple goal — helping students understand their performance and decide what to study next.
