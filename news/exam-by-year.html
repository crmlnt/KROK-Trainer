Exam by Year: Practice with KROK 1 Past Papers

A new way to prepare for KROK 1 is now available in KROK Trainer.

Exam by Year lets you take complete historical KROK 1 exam sets directly from Exam Mode. The first release includes Past Papers from 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2020 and 2023, with each session reproducing the full question set available for that year.

Unlike the standard By Subject mode, which creates a customizable exam from the KROK Trainer question bank, By Year is designed to simulate a complete historical paper.

Your Past Paper attempts are also saved separately in Exam History, making it easier to track your performance across different exam years.

More years may be added as reliable source material becomes available.

Exam by Year is available now for KROK 1.
