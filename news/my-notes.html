NEW FEATURE24 August 2026

Introducing My Notes: build your own KROK study library

KROK Trainer now lets registered students attach personal notes to individual questions and keep them together in a private, searchable study library synchronized with their account.

Take notes where the learning happens

While working in Practice Mode, a note icon is available directly on the question card. Open it to write a personal reminder, mnemonic, clarification or key concept without leaving the question you are studying.

If a note already exists, KROK Trainer marks the question so you can immediately recognize that you have saved something there before. Opening it again lets you edit the text or delete the note.

A personal library, not a separate notebook

The new My Notes area brings all saved notes together. Each entry keeps the note connected to its original KROK question and subject, turning scattered study reminders into an organized personal revision library.

My Notes at a glanceSearch your notes · Filter by KROK 1 or KROK 2 · Filter by subject · Reopen the original question · Edit or remove notes from your study workflow.

Private and cloud-synced

My Notes is an account feature. Notes are stored privately for the signed-in user and synchronized through the KROK Trainer cloud backend, allowing the same study library to remain available across devices.

This is intentionally different from local-only practice data: personal notes are designed to be something students can build over time and return to throughout their preparation.

Practice first, exam integrity preserved

Notes are designed as a study aid, not an exam aid. They are available in learning contexts such as Practice Mode, while an active Exam Mode session remains focused on testing without access to saved notes.

From answering questions to building knowledge

My Notes is part of a broader shift in KROK Trainer from a simple question runner toward a personal study system. Exam History preserves completed sessions, Statistics identifies performance patterns, Weak Subjects highlights areas that need attention, AI Explain supports understanding, and My Notes now gives students a place to preserve what they personally want to remember.
