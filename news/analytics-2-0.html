Introducing Analytics 2.0: A Smarter Way to Track Your KROK Preparation

Preparing for KROK takes more than answering hundreds of questions. Analytics 2.0 turns your completed Exam Mode sessions into clear, honest, subject-by-subject insights so you can decide where your next study hour matters most.

Introduction

When you are preparing for KROK 1 or KROK 2, it is easy to measure effort by volume alone: how many tests you finished this week, or whether your last score happened to cross 64%. Yet a single exam score rarely tells the whole story. You might score 70% overall while consistently missing Pathomorphology questions, or feel stuck at 61% without realizing your recent sessions are much stronger than your earliest attempts.

Today we are releasing Analytics 2.0, a redesigned Statistics experience built to help registered students understand how their exam preparation is evolving across subjects, across time periods, and within each individual discipline.

A clearer view of your progress

The updated Statistics dashboard brings your core Exam Mode indicators together in a clean, scannable layout. At a glance, four color-accented summary cards highlight your Completed Exams, Answered Questions, question-weighted Overall Accuracy, and Best Exam Score for the selected period.

Because KROK exams vary in length—from focused 25-question subject tests to full 150-question Past Papers—Analytics 2.0 calculates overall accuracy across all answered exam questions rather than averaging session percentages. That means a full-length simulation carries its true weight alongside shorter subject tests.

Discover your strengths and weaknesses

Below the overview and progress chart, Analytics 2.0 organizes your question-level results by medical discipline. Every question from completed Exam Mode sessions with full question details—including All Topics simulations and Past Paper exams where subject attribution is available—contributes to its corresponding subject.

Once you have answered at least 10 exam questions in a subject within the selected time window, that discipline appears in Weak Subjects and is classified against the 64% KROK passing benchmark:

  • Critical (<50%) — subjects where foundational review and mistake analysis should take priority.
  • Improve (50–63%) — subjects approaching the benchmark that need targeted reinforcement to reach passing level.
  • On track (≥64%) — subjects currently meeting or exceeding the 64% threshold.

Track your progress over time

All-time averages are helpful for the big picture, but they can also hide recent progress if you struggled during your first weeks of studying. Analytics 2.0 adds four temporal filters directly at the top of the Statistics page:

  • All Time — your complete validated Exam Mode history.
  • Last 90 Days — a quarterly view suited for semester-long exam preparation.
  • Last 30 Days — a monthly window to evaluate your current study routine.
  • Last 7 Days — a focused look at your most recent revision week.

Switching between time windows updates every section of the page—overall KPIs, the chronological progress chart, Weak Subjects, subject accuracy bars, and KROK 1 / KROK 2 summaries—instantly in your browser without reloading data from the server.

Go deeper with Subject Detail

Sometimes knowing your average in a subject is only the starting point. You may want to see whether your Anatomy or Internal Medicine accuracy has been climbing from exam to exam, or how far you currently stand from the 64% passing mark.

Every subject row now includes a dedicated Subject Detail view. Opening a subject displays:

  • Subject-specific KPIs — accuracy, answered questions, correct answers, and contributing exams for that discipline and KROK version.
  • 64% target distance — an explicit readout showing whether the subject meets the 64% threshold or how many percentage points remain.
  • Per-exam accuracy chart — a chronological chart plotting your accuracy in that subject across each eligible completed exam in the selected period.
  • Subject trend & period coverage — clear status information showing how recent responses compare with earlier ones and how complete the underlying period dataset is.

Reliable and transparent statistics

Study analytics are only useful when you can trust how the numbers are calculated. While designing Analytics 2.0, we focused on making sure every metric reflects verified examination data rather than noisy approximations:

Exam Mode focus

Statistics are calculated exclusively from completed Exam Mode sessions. Casual Practice Mode browsing stays separate so you can explore questions freely without worrying about lowering your exam metrics.

KROK 1 & KROK 2 separation

KROK 1 and KROK 2 analytics are tracked independently. If a subject appears in both examinations, its trends and Subject Detail views remain strictly separated by KROK version.

Honest trend thresholds

Subject trends (Improving, Stable, or Declining) require at least 40 validated responses in that subject and KROK version (comparing 20 older and 20 newer responses). Smaller samples are transparently labeled Not enough data.

Transparent historical coverage

Older exam sessions saved before question-level tracking was introduced still count toward your overall Exam Mode totals, while coverage notes clearly indicate which sessions include detailed subject attribution.

A tool for reflection, not a prediction Analytics 2.0 is designed to help you organize your study priorities and see how your Exam Mode results evolve over time. It does not predict official examination outcomes, and it works best when combined with active mistake review and consistent practice.
