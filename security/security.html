Security & Data Protection

KROK Trainer is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide its study and account features. We use simple, practical safeguards to help protect that information.

🔒

Secure connections

KROK Trainer uses HTTPS to encrypt communication between your browser and the website.

👤

Secure authentication

Account registration, sign-in and password management are handled through Supabase Auth.

🗄️

User data isolation

Database access policies are used to restrict authenticated users to data associated with their own account.

✉️

Account email protection

Account confirmation and password recovery emails are delivered through Resend using the official KROK Trainer domain.

🧠

Privacy-aware AI

AI Explain sends only the information required to generate an explanation of the selected KROK question.

🪶

Minimal data collection

KROK Trainer does not require your name, address, phone number, payment information or medical records to create an account.

PRIVACY BY SIMPLICITY

We keep data collection minimal.

KROK Trainer is an educational platform. We believe one of the simplest ways to protect personal information is not to collect information that the service does not need.

Creating an account is optional. You can use the core study features of KROK Trainer without registering.
